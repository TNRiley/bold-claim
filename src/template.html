What a measurement looks like

Nothing here reads a font's own declared metrics. A horizontal line is laid across the bowl of the o at its own mid height and the ink it crosses is measured; a vertical line through the middle of the same bowl gives the thin stroke. Cap height is the top of the H, x-height the top of the x. Every number is in units of a 1000-unit em, so a font drawn on a 2048-unit grid and one drawn on a 1000-unit grid can be compared directly. Pick any family below and the page draws the lines it was actually measured on.
